The idea
03 / 06

Start with the craving, not the restaurant.

If two people agree on the dish first, picking the place becomes much easier.

Swipe on food

People react naturally to what they feel like eating right now.

Match and move

Once they match, they can go straight to where to eat it.

CraveMatch turns indecision into a playful shared moment.
How it works
04 / 06

Four simple steps.

1. Create a room

One person starts the session and shares the room code.

2. Join the room

The second person enters the code on their own phone.

3. Swipe on dishes

Both people like or skip until the same food wins.

4. Pick the place

The app shows where to go next so the decision feels complete.

Simple enough to explain in seconds. Fun enough to remember.
